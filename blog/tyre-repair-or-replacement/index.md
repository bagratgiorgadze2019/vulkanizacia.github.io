When a tyre is damaged, the question is always the same — repair it or replace it? The right decision depends on the type and location of the damage.

## When a repair is possible

- A small puncture (nail, screw) in the tread area
- A puncture up to 6 mm in diameter
- The tyre is otherwise in good condition and not badly worn

## When it is better to replace

- Sidewall damage or a bulge
- A large cut or several punctures close together
- Worn tread (less than 1.6 mm)
- Old, cracked rubber

## How to know for sure

The best way is to have a technician inspect the tyre. Our mobile tyre service assesses the damage on the spot and tells you honestly whether a repair is enough or a replacement is better.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a repaired tyre safe?

Yes, if the damage is in the tread area and was repaired correctly. Sidewall damage cannot be repaired safely.

### How long does a repaired tyre last?

A properly done repair keeps the tyre airtight for the rest of its service life.
