Alloy rims can start leaking or crack over time — after hitting a pothole or because of corrosion. In many cases welding restores the rim without buying a new one.

## When welding is possible

- A crack or hole on the inner part of the rim
- A leak caused by corrosion or a small crack
- Aluminium and alloy rims

## When straightening is better

If the rim is bent (not cracked), it usually needs straightening rather than welding. Often both are done together.

## When it is better to replace

- A deep structural crack in the centre or on the spokes
- A badly deformed rim
- Damage that affects safety

## What we do

We inspect the rim, clean the damaged area, weld it and check that it is airtight. If needed, we straighten the rim as well — all on call in Tbilisi.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a welded rim safe?

Yes, if the crack is in the inner area and was welded correctly. Structural cracks in the centre are assessed case by case.

### Will the rim leak after welding?

A properly done weld stays airtight for a long time; we check the result before returning the wheel.
