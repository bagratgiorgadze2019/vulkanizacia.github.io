A tyre bursting or getting damaged while you drive is a common and dangerous situation. The key is not to panic and to act correctly. Here are 6 steps.

## 1. Stay calm and don't brake hard

Sudden steering or hard braking is dangerous. Hold the wheel firmly, slow down gradually and carefully move the car to the side of the road.

## 2. Switch on the hazard lights

Stop in a safe place, switch on the hazard lights and, if possible, set up the warning triangle — especially at night or on a highway.

## 3. Assess the damage

- A small puncture in the tread — usually repairable
- Sidewall damage or a large cut — the tyre usually has to be replaced
- A completely burst tyre — do not drive on it

## 4. Don't drive on a burst tyre

Driving on a burst or flat tyre damages the rim and is dangerous. It is better to solve the problem on the spot.

## 5. Fit the spare or call a mobile tyre service

If you have a spare wheel and are in a safe place, you can fit it. If not, call a mobile tyre service — we come to you and repair or replace the tyre on the spot.
