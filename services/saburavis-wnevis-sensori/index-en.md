## Why the pressure light comes on

- Pressure dropped in one of the tires — check the pressure first
- The sensor battery is dead (usually after several years)
- The sensor was damaged during a tire change or an impact
- A new sensor or new wheels are not programmed to the car

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a TPMS sensor?

A small sensor inside the wheel that measures tire pressure and sends it wirelessly to the car. In Georgia it is often called the tire “bluetooth”.

### Can you replace sensors on any car?

Not always — it depends on the make, model and year. Call us and we'll check.

### How long does a sensor last?

The sensor has a built-in battery that usually lasts several years.

### The light is on but the pressure is fine — what should I do?

It is probably a sensor or programming issue. Call us — we'll check it.
