## What we do on site

- Steam headlight restoration
- Hand polishing — sanding and polishing

- Side light and fog light bulbs

## Steam or hand polishing — which is better

Steam restoration suits badly yellowed and cloudy headlights: we first prepare the surface, then restore clarity with steam. Hand polishing suits lightly clouded headlights. We choose the method by the headlight's condition.

## Car bulb replacement

We replace headlight, side light and fog light bulbs — H1, H4, H7, H11 and other halogen and LED bulbs. We can bring a bulb for your car or fit yours. On some models the headlight or bumper has to come off to reach the bulb — we'll confirm when you call.

## When you need headlight restoration

- The headlights are yellow or cloudy
- The road is hard to see at night
- Before a technical inspection
- Before selling the car
