## Signs your brake pads need replacing

- Squeaking or grinding when braking
- The brake pad wear light is on
- Longer braking distance
- Vibration in the pedal or steering wheel when braking
- The car pulls to one side when braking

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does brake pad replacement cost?

It depends on your car's make and model, the pads and which axle is being done. Call us — we agree the price in advance.

### How often should brake pads be replaced?

It depends on driving style and roads. Check the pads roughly every 10,000 km and replace them when the friction material is down to about 3 mm.

### Do front or rear pads wear faster?

Front pads usually wear faster, because most of the braking load goes to the front axle. Pads are always replaced in pairs — on both wheels of one axle.

### Do I need new discs too?

Not always. A disc is replaced if it is badly worn, deeply scored or causes vibration. We check on site and tell you.

### Do you bring the brake pads?

Yes — we bring pads that fit your car, or fit the ones you bought.

### Do you come at night?
