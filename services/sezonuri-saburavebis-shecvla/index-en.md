## Frequently asked questions

### When should I fit winter tyres?

When the average daily temperature stays below about 7°C — in Tbilisi that is usually November. Fit them earlier if you are heading to mountain roads.

### Can you mount tyres on rims on site?

Yes — our mobile tyre service comes with all the equipment needed for mounting and balancing.

### How long does it take?

Usually about 30–60 minutes for four wheels, depending on whether the tyres need mounting on rims.
