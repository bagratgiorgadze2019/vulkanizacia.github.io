## Frequently asked questions

### Is a repaired tyre safe?

Yes, if the damage is in the tread area and the repair is done properly. A sidewall can't be safely repaired — it needs replacement.

### Do you have tyres for replacement?

Call with your size and car model and we'll advise on options and price.

### How long does a repair last?

A properly done repair holds its seal for a long time, within the tyre's remaining life.
