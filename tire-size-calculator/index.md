## How the calculator works

Overall diameter = rim diameter + 2 × sidewall height. Sidewall height = width × profile / 100. For example, 205/55 R16 has a sidewall of ≈ 113 mm and an overall diameter of ≈ 632 mm.

## The 3% rule

When changing tire size, the overall diameter should differ from the original by no more than 3% — otherwise the speedometer reading changes and ABS and stability control may not work as designed.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does the tire size calculator show?

The overall diameter and sidewall height of two sizes, the difference in percent and your real speed when the speedometer shows 100 km/h.

### How much can a new size differ?

Ideally the overall diameter should differ by no more than 3%, and the size should be approved by the car manufacturer.

### Can you fit a different tire size?

Yes — if the size is acceptable, we bring the tires and fit them on site. Call us for help choosing.
