## How to choose

- Summer — if you use separate winter tires in winter
- All-season — if you mostly drive in the city and rarely go to the mountains
- Low-profile — for sporty driving, but easier to damage on potholes
- When the temperature stays above 7°C, switch from winter to summer tires

Help with the size: [How to read a tire size](https://vulkanizacia.ge/blog/how-to-read-tyre-size/)

## When you need a tire replacement

- The tire burst or the sidewall is damaged — this kind of damage can't be repaired
- The puncture is too large or on the sidewall
- A bulge appeared after hitting a pothole or a kerb
- The tread is worn — below 3 mm on summer tires, 4 mm on winter tires
- The tire has cracks, or it is over 6 years old and the rubber has hardened
- The winter or summer season has arrived
- The tire wears unevenly or vibration remains after balancing
- One tire is damaged and you need a matching one for the same axle

## Frequently asked questions

### Will all-season tires cope with winter?

For Tbilisi's mild winter, good all-season tires are often enough, but on snow, ice and mountain roads winter tires are much better.

### When should I switch back to summer tires?

When the average daily temperature stays above 7°C — in Tbilisi usually in late March or April.
