## What makes a winter tyre different

- Soft rubber that stays flexible in the cold
- Deep tread and fine sipes for grip on snow and ice
- Better braking on cold and wet roads
- M+S marking or the mountain-snowflake symbol (3PMSF)

More: [Winter tyres — when to change?](https://vulkanizacia.ge/blog/winter-tyres-when-to-change/) · [Seasonal tyre change on site](https://vulkanizacia.ge/services/sezonuri-saburavebis-shecvla/index-en.html)

## When you need a tyre replacement

- The tyre burst or the sidewall is damaged — this kind of damage can't be repaired
- The puncture is too large or on the sidewall
- A bulge appeared after hitting a pothole or a kerb
- The tread is worn — below 3 mm on summer tyres, 4 mm on winter tyres
- The tyre has cracks, or it is over 6 years old and the rubber has hardened
- The winter or summer season has arrived
- The tyre wears unevenly or vibration remains after balancing
- One tyre is damaged and you need a matching one for the same axle

## Frequently asked questions

### When should I fit winter tyres?

When the average daily temperature stays below about 7°C — in Tbilisi that is usually November. Fit them earlier if you are heading to mountain roads.

### Do winter tyres have to go on all four wheels?

Yes — on all four, so the car stays stable when braking and cornering.

### Do you have used winter tyres?

Yes, we have both new and used winter tyres. Used ones should have at least 4 mm of tread.
